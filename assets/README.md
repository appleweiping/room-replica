# Texture provenance

`ASSET_SOURCES.json` records the original URLs, authors, licenses, byte sizes and hashes of the downloaded PBR maps. Each download was verified against the MD5 checksum supplied by the Poly Haven API, with SHA-256 additionally recorded for project integrity checks.

## Downloaded PBR sets

| Set | Files | Reference scale | Grain direction | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Red Oak Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/red_oak_veneer) by Jenelle van Heerden | `red_oak_veneer_*_2k.jpg` | 1 m across | Horizontal in image | Door leaves, wardrobe and cabinet panels with an amber stain in the shader |
| [Kitchen Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/kitchen_wood) by Rob Tuytel | `kitchen_wood_*_2k.jpg` | 0.6 m across | Vertical in image | Narrow furniture rails and contrasting solid wood elements |

Both sets are available under [CC0-1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license), including redistribution. Original downloaded pixels are preserved. The metadata `dimensions` values are reported in millimetres by the provider.

For Blender, load `diff` as sRGB. Load `rough` and `nor_gl` as Non-Color data. Feed the OpenGL normal through a Normal Map node. Finished varnished wood benefits from a restrained normal strength and a separate coat layer. The builder already authors per-face UVs with the grain aligned to each part's longest direction; no extra 90-degree rotation is required. Amber shader tint matches the photographed finish and is exported as a glTF color factor. Both texture sets are included for further editing; the final native scene uses red oak diffuse and normal maps with authored roughness and coat settings.

The rose, carpet, generic notice and fallback blue images are project-authored, reproducible script outputs. The final `blue_floral_art.png` is AI-assisted, reference-informed albedo artwork; it is committed as an input asset and is not regenerated by those scripts. Its complete prompt, provenance and repeat-boundary limitation are recorded in [`docs/fabric-generation.md`](../docs/fabric-generation.md). None of these images are Poly Haven downloads.
