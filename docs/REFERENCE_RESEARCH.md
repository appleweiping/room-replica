No official, publicly accessible dimensioned floor plan or room-number schedule was found in the sources searched. No room-specific width, depth, ceiling height, window dimensions or cabinet dimensions were verified. The official page offers example tours rather than a dimensioned drawing. It also warns that room details may change.

The photographs show a private sleeping/study area, individual kitchenette, separate sink niche and two wood doors. This is **visually consistent with an efficiency arrangement**, but the exact apartment type is an inference, not a verified room assignment. No unpictured bathroom, neighboring unit or hidden service space is reconstructed as if observed.

## Scale and reconstruction policy

Use the 80-inch bed length as an anchor, then compare multiple photographs to estimate the shell and furniture. Store the final estimated dimensions in the scene configuration and manifest. Do not back-calculate the photographed room to 335 ft²; that could incorrectly include the paired unit and bathroom. Uncertainty is also present in smartphone perspective, occlusion, movable objects and hidden surfaces.

This is a **photograph-guided, manually parameterized reconstruction**, not a laser scan, surveyed digital twin, or a photogrammetric measurement. The model may be useful for visual review, but dimensions should be measured on site before planning a furniture purchase or construction.
